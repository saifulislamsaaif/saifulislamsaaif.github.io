About

I'm a senior back-end engineer with over 16 years of experience building systems where correctness and performance matter, across warehouse management, medical devices, and financial infrastructure.

Today I work as a Software Consultant at Sigma Technology Software Solutions, assigned to IMI Supply Chain Solutions. I develop their warehouse management system in C#/.NET and Oracle PL/SQL. My work covers the pick order release engine, data-warehouse export integrations, warehouse-automation (robotics) integrations, and internal tooling. A highlight has been building a simulation and testing framework that runs realistic warehouse scenarios, so the release logic can be optimized and refactored with confidence.

Earlier, as a System Consultant at ALTEN Sweden, I contributed to a large-scale internal platform at Meta (via Sigma Connectivity) and built cloud-native back-end APIs for the NIOX VERO medical device ecosystem. Before that, I led development of a blockchain-based proxy voting platform for NuArca, built on IBM Hyperledger Fabric for large financial institutions.

I use AI-assisted development tools such as Claude Code throughout my workflow, from problem analysis and design to implementation, review, and testing, while owning the technical decisions and verification.
